Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Fondant Queue Craft ("the App") handles information. The App is developed by Rosewood Studio.

1. No Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. No user accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, and no advertising identifiers are used.

2. Local Storage Only

Game progress (unlocked levels) and settings (music, sound effects, haptics) are saved locally on your device using iOS standard storage. This data never leaves your device and is not shared with anyone.

3. Offline Operation

The App works fully offline. No internet connection is required to play. The App does not connect to any server or external service.

4. No Third-Party Services

The App contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising networks, no social sharing, and no in-app purchases.

5. No Tracking

The App does not track your activity, location, or behavior. There are no tracking pixels, beacons, or fingerprinting mechanisms.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is rated 4+ and is safe for children. No personal data is collected from anyone, including children.

7. Your Rights

Since no personal data is collected, no rights need to be exercised. You can reset all local data by deleting and reinstalling the App.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
