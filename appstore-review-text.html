App Store Localizable Text
Match each bakery ticket before it expires. Choose base, cream, filling, and decor to build cakes across 50 escalating levels.
cake,fondant,queue,craft,order,bakery,cream,filling,decoration,levels,offline,puzzle,time
Fondant Queue Craft is a bakery puzzle where every ticket asks for a specific cake. Read the order card, pick the right base, cream, filling, and decoration, then submit the cake before the ticket expires.

Each level starts with an order card listing what each ticket needs: a base (Chocolate, Strawberry, Matcha, Cheese, Rainbow, or Tiramisu), a cream (White, Pink, Blue, Yellow, Green, or Cocoa), a filling (Vanilla, Berry, Nuts, or Custard), and a decoration (Candles, Flowers, Stars, Hearts, or Gold). Switch between the four category tabs at the bottom of the screen, tap the option that matches one of the open tickets, and watch the cake preview update in the center.

Tickets keep arriving while you work. A countdown bar on each ticket shows how much time remains before it expires. When your cake matches a ticket, tap Submit Cake to complete the order. If no ticket matches, the app tells you to try again. Complete enough orders before too many tickets expire to finish the level and unlock the next one.

Early levels teach one category at a time. Level 1 focuses on Base only, Level 2 on Cream, Level 3 on Filling, and Level 4 on Decoration. From Level 7 onward, all four categories are required for every ticket. As levels progress, more tickets can stack on screen at once (up to 4), the per-order timer shrinks from 20 seconds down to 10 seconds, and fewer expired tickets are allowed per level.

Features:
- Read bakery order tickets and build matching cakes across 50 levels.
- Choose from 6 bases, 6 creams, 4 fillings, and 5 decorations.
- Switch between Base, Cream, Filling, and Decor tabs to select each component.
- Watch the cake preview update as you add each layer.
- Manage up to 4 simultaneous tickets with countdown timers.
- Submit cakes to match any open ticket before it expires.
- Track progress with the Level Select screen showing LOCKED, OPEN, and DONE states.
- Browse 50 levels in a paginated 2-column grid, 10 levels per page.
- Pause overlay with Resume, Restart, and Menu options.
- Settings panel to toggle Music, Sound Effects, and Haptics.
- Fully offline; progress and settings saved locally on device.

Gameplay Fact Card Summary
Core screen: Bakery background with cake preview center, order card top with countdown bars, category tabs (Base/Cream/Filling/Decor) and option grid at bottom, Submit Cake button.
Controls: Tap category tab to switch; tap option to select; tap Submit Cake to match against open tickets; tap Pause for overlay.
Goal: Build a cake that matches an open ticket (base + cream + filling + decor) and submit before the timer expires. Complete enough orders to pass the level.
Fail: Too many tickets expire before the orders target is reached.
Progression: 50 levels, sequential unlock, local save. 1 category (L1-4) to all 4 (L7+). 2 to 4 simultaneous tickets. 20s to 10s timer.
