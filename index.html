About Fondant Queue Craft

Fondant Queue Craft is a bakery puzzle where every ticket asks for a specific cake. Each order lists a base, a cream, a filling, and a decoration. Read the ticket, switch between the four category tabs, tap the matching option, and submit the cake before the countdown bar runs out.

The game spans 50 levels with progressive difficulty. Levels 1–4 teach one category at a time (Base, Cream, Filling, Decor). From level 7 onward, all four categories are required for every ticket. More tickets stack on screen at once, the per-order timer shrinks, and fewer expired tickets are allowed.

Six bases (Chocolate, Strawberry, Matcha, Cheese, Rainbow, Tiramisu), six creams (White, Pink, Blue, Yellow, Green, Cocoa), four fillings (Vanilla, Berry, Nuts, Custard), and five decorations (Candles, Flowers, Stars, Hearts, Gold) give every ticket a unique combination. Up to four tickets can be active simultaneously, each with its own countdown timer.

How to Play

1

Start a Level

Tap Start Order on the main menu to continue from your highest unlocked level, or visit Levels to pick any unlocked stage.

2

Read the Order Card

The order card at the top shows open tickets. Each ticket lists the base, cream, filling, and decoration it needs, with a countdown bar.

3

Pick a Category

Tap the Base, Cream, Filling, or Decor tab at the bottom to see the available options in that category.

4

Tap the Matching Option

Select the option that matches one of the open tickets. The cake preview in the center updates as you build.

5

Submit the Cake

Tap Submit Cake to check the cake against all open tickets. A matching ticket is completed and the next one arrives.

6

Beat the Clock

Complete enough orders before too many tickets expire to finish the level and unlock the next one.
