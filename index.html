About

Fondant Queue Craft is a bakery puzzle where every ticket asks for a specific cake: a base, a cream, a filling, and a decoration. Read the order card, switch between the four category tabs, tap the option that matches the ticket, and submit the cake before the timer runs out.

Tickets keep arriving while you work. As levels progress, more tickets can stack on screen at once, the per-order timer shrinks, and fewer expired tickets are allowed. Stay calm, match the right cake to the right ticket, and keep the bakery moving.

How to Play

  1. Tap Start Order on the main menu to begin the next unlocked level.
  2. Read the order card at the top: it lists the base, cream, filling, and decoration each ticket needs.
  3. Tap a category tab (Base, Cream, Filling, Decor) to see the available options.
  4. Tap the option that matches one of the open tickets. The cake preview updates as you build.
  5. When the cake matches a ticket, tap Submit Cake to complete the order and move to the next.
  6. Complete enough orders before too many tickets expire to finish the level and unlock the next one.
